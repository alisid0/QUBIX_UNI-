## Governance

The complete topic names, role, ordering and phase practicals are kept in the
canonical structured list so the Factory renders the same catalogue recorded by
the curriculum. A catalogue entry is not a board, source selection, approval or
release. Each topic becomes learner content only after its dependencies and one
observable understanding are defined and its board passes the review protocol.

Current phase 3 is highlighted in the Factory because the present board is the
Pre-Intern data-literacy capstone: cases, variables and the tabular dataset they
form. Passing all phase 0–3 prerequisites earns promotion to Data Intern. Later
phases remain planned and locked.
