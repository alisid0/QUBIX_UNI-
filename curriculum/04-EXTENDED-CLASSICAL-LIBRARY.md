# Extended Classical Mathematics Library

Status: **Candidate library — no title is approved automatically**
Purpose: give each learning block (BB) several strong classical perspectives before Qubix chooses its explanation, examples and interactions.

## The governing rule

Breadth changes the quality of selection, not the speed of production. For each BB, the founder reads the relevant short source extracts, tests the proposed interaction, amends the teaching and approves or rejects it. The next BB remains locked until that decision is recorded.

The books below are candidates. The six books in [Foundational Mathematics Books](./03-FOUNDATIONAL-BOOKS.md) remain the current anchors.

## What “classical meets modern” means

Qubix should preserve the intellectual move in a classical text, not reproduce its page layout. A selected passage can become a small sequence:

1. a question or prediction;
2. one manipulable object;
3. a visible consequence;
4. the classical explanation in a short, carefully attributed extract or adaptation;
5. a misconception check;
6. one act of recall or reconstruction.

A BB may compare two authors when their explanations genuinely illuminate different sides of the same idea. Historical terminology, obsolete notation and outdated cultural claims must be labelled and contextualised, not silently copied.

## Legal status labels

- `ANCHOR`: on the current core shelf, still subject to passage-level provenance.
- `ANCHOR_LATER`: approved only as a possible later-stage anchor, not beginner material.
- `SUPPLEMENTARY`: optional investigation material; never controls the dependency map.
- `EDITION_REVIEW`: promising candidate; exact text, illustrations and territory status must be logged before use.
- `TRANSLATION_REVIEW`: original may be old, but this translation and its editorial material require separate review.
- `HOLD_UK_REVIEW`: do not adapt for release until UK status is resolved. In particular, L. C. Karpinski died in 1956, so ordinary UK life-plus-70 analysis requires care even where a US edition is public domain.

## Admission test for a candidate book

A candidate moves into active BB research only when all answers are recorded:

1. Which exact learning objective does it serve?
2. What does it explain better than the current anchor?
3. Which exact edition, chapter and pages will be used?
4. Are the text, translation, annotations and illustrations separately clear for the launch territories?
5. What must be modernised, corrected or contextualised?
6. What interaction makes the learner think rather than merely click?
7. Has the founder read the selected material and approved its use?
