## Objective

The learner distinguishes path distance from directed displacement, then
represents a vector by an arrow whose length and direction both carry meaning.

## Source record

- Urone and Hinrichs, OpenStax *Physics* (2020), §§2.1 and 5.1
- https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/2-1-relative-motion-distance-and-displacement
- Creative Commons Attribution; TEA attribution required

All learner wording, examples, diagrams and controls are Qubix-original.

## Structure

1. Compare circular arc distance with straight start-to-finish displacement.
2. Name direction with compass points and angles from east.
3. Build and compare arrows carrying both magnitude and direction.
