Status: `AI_DRAFT`, gated

## Objective

The learner adds vectors graphically by placing them head to tail and identifies
the resultant as the single vector from the first tail to the final head.

## Source record

- Paul Peter Urone and Roger Hinrichs, OpenStax *Physics* (2020), §5.1
- https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/5-1-vector-addition-and-subtraction-graphical-methods
- Creative Commons Attribution; Texas Education Agency attribution required

All learner wording, examples, diagrams and controls are Qubix-original.

## Structure

1. Translate an arrow without changing its magnitude or direction.
2. Add perpendicular vectors by placing them head to tail.
3. Draw the resultant and investigate equal opposite vectors cancelling.
