# Wikimedia Source and Attribution Policy

Status: **Active authoring rule**  
Recorded: 2026-08-21

Wikipedia is useful to Qubix as a concept map, secondary explanation source and
route to references. It is not public-domain text. English Wikipedia text is
normally available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International licence, with additional terms.

## Required record

Any Qubix source entry that quotes or adapts Wikipedia must include:

- article title and “Wikipedia contributors” attribution;
- exact permanent revision URL;
- access date;
- `CC BY-SA 4.0` label and licence URL;
- a clear indication of whether material was quoted, shortened, paraphrased or
  otherwise changed;
- separate licence verification for every image, audio or other media asset.

The Factory must display those fields beside the source. A plain link to a live,
changing article is not enough for an auditable board.

## Authoring boundary

- Prefer original Qubix explanations based on facts checked across stronger
  sources rather than copying Wikipedia prose.
- Use short attributed excerpts only when the wording itself is valuable.
- Treat Wikipedia citations as leads; open and assess the underlying source
  before it becomes the authority for a learner-facing claim.
- Never assume a Wikimedia image has the same licence as the article text.
- Never copy a diagram when a deterministic Qubix SVG, canvas or simulation can
  teach the structure more clearly.
- Preserve ShareAlike obligations for adaptations and do not add restrictions
  that conflict with the source licence.
