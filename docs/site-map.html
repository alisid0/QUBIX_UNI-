Walking it

The order to click, to see everything once

  1. Open /. That is the landing production serves. Open Ask Qubix bottom right and ask it where to start, how long it takes, and whether it is finished.
  2. Go to ?mode=game. Note which missions are locked: the sequence is enforced, so a fresh browser can only open the first.
  3. Open ?mode=game&mission=store for the floor plan, and find the rooms the new missions sit in.
  4. Read ?mode=game&mission=shared-book&chapter=1&session=1 and walk forward. Every session has an Ask Qubix of its own.
  5. Play the missions in roster order. To reach a later one without playing everything, run this in the browser console first:
    localStorage.setItem('qx.superstore.progress.v1', JSON.stringify({completed:['checkout','classify-data','table-grain','missing-data','units-measurement','uom','rate-desk','duplicate-records','data-lineage','distribution-desk','zone-price','sampling-desk','sql-console','region-grain','join-grain'],xp:0}))
  6. Finish sql-console and take the link it offers at the end. That is the only in-course route to the data console.
  7. In ?lab=sql, run the five starter queries, then break one deliberately and check the error is readable.
  8. Come back through each of the three links in the console header and confirm they land where they say.

Watch for the things guards cannot see: prose that reads badly, a step that is obvious in the code and confusing on screen, and any moment where you do not know what to do next.
